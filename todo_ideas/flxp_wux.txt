* keyboard cues in dialogs: Wine's buttons, statics and tabs (comctl32 button.c, static.c, tab.c) draw their mnemonics
	without asking WM_QUERYUISTATE, so dialog labels stay underlined; XP hides them until Alt (the UI state is there now:
	defwnd, the menu band, rebar)

* flicker on resize (a lead, not confirmed): wux windows use the default ForgetGravity, so the X server drops a window's
	contents on every resize, and a glass (ARGB) window's transparent background shows before wux repaints; try NorthWest
	gravity (and a background of None) and watch a resize under picom

* start panel, styled: XP puts the All Programs arrow after the width of "All &Programs" (moreprog.cpp measures the
	ampersand), about 6 px further right than ours in every style (WinXP-193504.png, Lunar); kept so the Luna and Longhorn
	panels stay as they are. XP's pinned items have a second line (the program's description) that ours lack

* 5048 and 5112 use the `concept` luxnav preset for now (set in their themes); presets of their own in share/navbar/vision.ini
	when they diverge; Display Properties still offers Small/Big/Concept for them although the Vista era has no header sizes
