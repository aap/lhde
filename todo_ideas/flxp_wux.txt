* fonts left on DEFAULT_GUI_FONT (Tahoma 8 under every style): the places pane's filter box and the address bar (4074-dwm.png
	shows "Filter by" in Segoe UI under Jade; the box is GR_BOX_H high, too low for Segoe UI 9 as it stands), dialogs (MS

* keyboard cues: SPI_GETKEYBOARDCUES answers TRUE, so every wux menu and the menu band underline their mnemonics all the
	time; XP and the 4074 screenshots hide them until Alt or a keyboard-opened menu (luxwm's window menu does that already)

* flicker on resize (a lead, not confirmed): wux windows use the default ForgetGravity, so the X server drops a window's
	contents on every resize, and a glass (ARGB) window's transparent background shows before wux repaints; try NorthWest
	gravity (and a background of None) and watch a resize under picom

* start panel, styled: XP puts the All Programs arrow after the width of "All &Programs" (moreprog.cpp measures the
	ampersand), about 6 px further right than ours in every style (WinXP-193504.png, Lunar); kept so the Luna and Longhorn
	panels stay as they are. XP's pinned items have a second line (the program's description) that ours lack
* menus: user32's submenu arrow (OBM_MNARROW, 5x9, 1 px from the item's end) is bigger than XP's classic one (Marlett,
	4x7, about 8 px from the menu's edge; WinXP-193221.png): every wux menu

* 5048 and 5112 use the `concept` luxnav preset for now (set in their themes); presets of their own in data/navbar/vision.ini
	when they diverge; Display Properties still offers Small/Big/Concept for them although the Vista era has no header sizes
