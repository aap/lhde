## resizing
* maximize vertically when resize-dragging window border to edge of maximize-area
* maximize when move-dragging window to top edge,
	maximize to left/right half when dragging to left/half edge

* alt-space S to resize works really well for one direction, but diagonal is hard to get right. should be easier
* dialogs like "Display Properties" currently resizable. not sure whose fault that is, but at the wm layer this should be handled correctly
