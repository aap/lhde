## what other opinionated desktops ship (Enlightenment's modules, Haiku's apps; a survey, 2026-11-01)
* neither ships an image viewer, PDF viewer or editor in the core: E leaves them to separate EFL apps (ephoto, rage, ecrire,
	terminology); Haiku's are ShowImage, StyledEdit, Terminal, BePDF (small single-window programs, no toolbar clutter: the
	closest models for an XP-era image viewer, Notepad-class editor and mupf)
* what lhde lacks that both have and a user misses first: a launcher with search (E's "everything": apps, files, settings,
	calculator, clipboard history; flxp-startmenu's search box is the seed), a volume mixer and a notification history, a
	screenshot tool (E's shot: small, and the lab wants one), a clipboard manager, a screen lock and logout/suspend menu,
	a themed terminal; Haiku's Expander (archives), ActivityMonitor (Task Manager), DiskProbe
* Haiku's lesson for the annotations: People, Mail and bookmarks are attributed files that Tracker and Find query; keep the
	catalogue idea aimed at that (data as attributed files), not only at speed
* E's theming, as a concept: one theme skins WM, shell and every app; an app may add or override a few parts
	(elm_theme_extension/overlay) and pick a per-widget style name. The entry point to copy: one macro (ELM_MAIN) and a
	runnable one-file example per widget
