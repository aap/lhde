# some programs that would fit the system and are useful

## text editor

* luxpad first: ReactOS's notepad (base/applications/notepad, 3.3k lines of C on the stock Edit control, GPL-2+/LGPL-2.1+)
	on wux via apps/; wux lacks FindText/ReplaceText/ChooseFont (write them on wux's dialog manager: three functions, two
	templates), DragAcceptFiles, Get/SetWindowPlacement, file mapping (POSIX), IsTextUnicode; drop printing.c and PageSetup
* then luxpad++ on SciTE (permissive licence; platform code behind Platform.h and GUI, win32 and gtk frontends over one src/):
	Scintilla's win32 variant on wux's GDI (SurfaceGDI only, DISABLE_D2D, OLE drag-drop stubbed, IME is no-ops anyway), the
	SciTEWin frontend on wux; needs real RegisterClipboardFormat and the remaining GDI calls, CreateProcess/CreatePipe shimmed
	for the tool runner; Scintilla and Lexilla are not in ~/othersrc/scite yet (expected beside it). This is the test of wux's
	drawing path at volume (owner-drawn text, caret, timers, popup list, DIB sections, alpha) that flxp's stock controls are not
* not Notepad++: GPL-3 (incompatible with lhde's GPL-2) and 159k lines of raw Win32 (docking, subclassing, COM, plugins)

## what other opinionated desktops ship (Enlightenment's modules, Haiku's apps; a survey, 2026-11-01)
* neither ships an image viewer, PDF viewer or editor in the core: E leaves them to separate EFL apps (ephoto, rage, ecrire,
	terminology); Haiku's are ShowImage, StyledEdit, Terminal, BePDF (small single-window programs, no toolbar clutter: the
	closest models for an XP-era image viewer, Notepad-class editor and mupf)
* what lhde lacks that both have and a user misses first: a launcher with search (E's "everything": apps, files, settings,
	calculator, clipboard history; flxp-startmenu's search box is the seed), a volume mixer and a notification history,
	a clipboard manager, a screen lock and logout/suspend menu,
	a themed terminal; Haiku's Expander (archives), ActivityMonitor (Task Manager), DiskProbe
* Haiku's lesson for the annotations: People, Mail and bookmarks are attributed files that Tracker and Find query; keep the
	catalogue idea aimed at that (data as attributed files), not only at speed
* E's theming, as a concept: one theme skins WM, shell and every app; an app may add or override a few parts
	(elm_theme_extension/overlay) and pick a per-widget style name. The entry point to copy: one macro (ELM_MAIN) and a
	runnable one-file example per widget
