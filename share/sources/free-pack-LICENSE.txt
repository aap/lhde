lhde-resources-free-1: the free files of lhde, the Longhorn desktop environment: ReactOS 0.4.16's and four wallpapers

WHAT THIS IS

The files lhde takes from ReactOS 0.4.16's boot CD (ReactOS-0.4.16-i386.iso, https://reactos.org; the release:
https://sourceforge.net/projects/reactos/files/ReactOS/0.4.16/), unmodified, by the rules of lhde's
share/sources/reactos.ini (here as reactos/source.ini):

  reactos/system32/    shell32.dll, browseui.dll, comctl32.dll, themeui.dll, explorer.exe
  reactos/themes/      the visual styles Lunar, Modern, Mizu and Lautus
  reactos/fonts/       40 fonts under the Windows file names (below)

and the wallpapers of lhde's themes for those styles, four photos from Pexels, by share/sources/free.ini (free/source.ini):

  free/wallpapers/     the photos and LICENSE.txt, which names each one's photographer and Pexels page

MANIFEST lists every file with its sha256, its size and its path on the CD (the photos: their Pexels file names).
`lhde-import --pack` puts them into lhde's Windows tree (`make install-resources-free` downloads this pack and does that);
`make free-pack` in lhde's source tree makes the pack again from the CD and the photos (lhde-pack --reproducible --sources
reactos,free), byte for byte.
